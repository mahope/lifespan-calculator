# Find Forsikring on Levetidsberegner

Decision on 23 November 2026: decline activation.

The existing Partner-ads card appears only after a personal calculation. It sits
below the life-in-weeks chart and invites the reader to estimate how much life
insurance their family needs. Although the coverage formula uses household
finances independently of the lifespan estimate, the result-only placement can
make the offer feel like advice prompted by the reader's estimated remaining
time. Replacing the tracking link in that location would carry that implication
over to Adtraction.

A stand-alone section about reviewing everyday policies could avoid that
implication if it were accessible without taking the calculation, outside the
result flow and independent of all calculator inputs. The Adtraction approval
export from 21 November 2026 lists Find Forsikring (program 1873805482) for
minberegner.dk only. The same export lists zero approved programs for
levetidsberegner.dk. There is no verified channel-specific approval or tracking
link for Levetidsberegner.

This decision changes documentation only. The existing Partner-ads placement is
a separate editorial concern. No Adtraction click baseline was measured, and no
redirect was tested for the unverified channel.
